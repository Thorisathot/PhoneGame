Ashen Vigil privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Ashen Vigil ("the game") is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (gold, gear, upgrades, settings and the player name you choose) is saved on your device only. We do not receive it. You can erase it at any time in Settings > Erase progress, or by uninstalling the game or clearing the site data in your browser.

Online account, realms and cloud save

When you play online, the game creates an anonymous account for you automatically using Google Firebase. No email or real name is needed. We store: an account ID, the player name and portrait you choose, the realm (server) you belong to, a copy of your game progress (cloud save), your leaderboard records, your damage against the weekly world boss, and your guild membership. If you create a transfer code, a generated code and password are linked to your account so you can restore progress on another device.

Other players in your realm can see your player name, portrait, leaderboard records, world boss damage and guild. This data is stored by Google Firebase (Google Cloud). See Firebase privacy and security.

Guild chat

Guild members can send short text messages to their guild. Messages are visible to members of that guild and are stored by Firebase. Messages are filtered for offensive words. You can report a message, which sends it to us for review, and you can block a player so you no longer see their messages. We may remove messages and players that break the rules.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We never see your payment card details. To deliver and restore purchases, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, the products you buy and basic device information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob (for example, to double gold after a run). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. The website version of the game shows no ads.

Fonts and website hosting

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web request, these services receive your IP address and basic browser information. See Google's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your account

In the game, open the Play screen, tap Realm, then Account, then Delete my account. This permanently deletes your online account, cloud save, leaderboard records, world boss damage and guild membership, and erases progress on your device. You can also ask us to delete your account by email; see how to delete your account. Reports you sent and messages you posted in a guild may remain until the guild is removed.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights and contact

For questions, to request a copy of your data, or to ask RevenueCat to delete purchase records linked to your anonymous ID, contact us at the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page.

We may update this policy. The latest version is always at this address.
