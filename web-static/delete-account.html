Delete your Ashen Vigil account

Ashen Vigil, by Thorisathot

Delete it in the game (instant)

  1. Open Ashen Vigil and go to the Play screen.
  2. Tap Realm, then the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your online account, cloud save, leaderboard records, world boss damage and guild membership, and erases progress on your device.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the game installed, email the developer address shown on the game's Google Play page with the subject "Delete my account", and include your player name, realm and, if you have one, your transfer code. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: account ID, player name and portrait, cloud save, leaderboard records, world boss damage, guild membership, transfer code.

Kept: purchase records that Google Play and RevenueCat must keep for tax and refunds, and chat messages already posted in a guild until that guild is removed. Nothing else is kept.
